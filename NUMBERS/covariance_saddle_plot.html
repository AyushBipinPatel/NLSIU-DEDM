lifted points on z = xy surface height at centroid, E(X)E(Y) cloud average height, E(XY) dimension line = covariance
At r = 0 the cloud spreads evenly into all four quadrants around the centroid, so the two markers sit almost flush. As r rises toward 1, the cloud concentrates in the quadrants where x and y move together, and the average height climbs above the surface height at the centroid. The dimensioned gap is E(XY) minus E(X)E(Y), read directly off the drawing.
